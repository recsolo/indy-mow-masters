How to set out leaves for Indianapolis pickup

  1. Use large plastic trash bags for leaves, following Indianapolis DPW’s bag instructions.
  2. Set them out by 7 a.m. on your collection day, 3–5 feet from the cart pickup area. Keep sidewalks and rights-of-way clear.
  3. Keep loose piles out of the street. Confirm dates and instructions with your own hauler if you’re outside Indianapolis DPW service, including households using a town or private hauler.
