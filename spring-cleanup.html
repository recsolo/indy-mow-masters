Spring Cleanup Sign-Up Questions

Does signing up cost anything or commit me to anything?

No. Adding your name is free and there's no obligation. We contact you in late winter with a date and a price, and you decide then.

What does a spring cleanup include?

Winter debris and branch removal, dead plant and annual removal, bed edging, general raking, and blowing hard surfaces clean. Each job is tailored to the yard — tell us what you'd like prioritized. More detail on the yard cleanup page.

When do spring cleanups happen in Indianapolis?

Typically March through May, depending on the weather. We start as soon as the ground is workable and schedule the list in the order people signed up.

How much does a spring cleanup cost?

It depends on yard size, how much debris built up over winter, and which services you want. You get an exact price before any work begins, and haul-away is included in the quote.

Can I add mulch, mowing, or gutter cleaning?

Yes. Tick the extras on the sign-up sheet — fresh mulch, 2027 mowing, one-story gutter cleaning, or hand weeding — and we'll quote them together.

I need something done before spring. Do I use this sheet?

No — for anything this season, use the regular quote form or call or text (317) 514-2861. Fall work like gutter cleaning and leaf cleanup is booking now.
