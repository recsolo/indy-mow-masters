How It Works

Four Steps, One Visit

Same process on every home, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

01

Clear Every Run

We hand-scoop the leaves, shingle grit, seed pods, and packed sludge out of every gutter run around the house — not just the sections you can see from the driveway.

02

Clear the Downspouts

Most gutter failures are actually downspout clogs. We check each one and clear whatever's blocking it, top to bottom, until the line runs free.

03

Flush & Verify

We run water through the system and watch it exit the downspouts. If it doesn't drain properly, we're not finished — you get proof it works, not just our word.

04

Clean Up & Haul Off

Debris gets bagged and taken with us. Driveway, walkways, patio, and landscaping are left clean — you shouldn't be able to tell we were there except by the gutters.

Why It Matters

What Clogged Gutters Actually Cost You

Gutter cleaning is cheap. The things it prevents are not.

Foundation & Basement Water

A blocked gutter dumps roof runoff straight down against the foundation instead of carrying it away. In Indianapolis clay soil, that's the single most common source of a wet basement and settling cracks.

Fascia & Soffit Rot

Water standing in a packed gutter wicks backward into the fascia board it's mounted to. By the time you see paint bubbling or a sagging gutter line, you're looking at carpentry, not a cleaning.

Ice Dams in Winter

Debris-packed gutters can't drain during a January thaw, so meltwater refreezes at the roof edge and backs up under the shingles. Clearing them in late fall is the cheapest ice-dam insurance there is.

Pests & Nesting

Wet leaf sludge in a warm gutter is prime habitat for mosquitoes, carpenter ants, and nesting rodents looking for a way into the attic through the soffit. Clean gutters give them nothing to work with.

FAQ

Gutter Cleaning Questions

Straight answers about pricing, scheduling, and what we will and won't take on.

Do you clean gutters on two-story homes?

No — one-story homes only. Two-story and multi-level work needs taller ladders and fall-protection equipment we don't carry. We'd rather turn the job down than take one we can't do safely.

How much does gutter cleaning cost?

$99 to start for a small one-story home, $129 medium, $159 large. Final price depends on linear footage and debris volume, and we confirm it before any work begins.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Twice a year for most Indianapolis homes — late spring after seeds and blossoms drop, and late fall once the leaves are down. Homes under heavy tree cover often need the fall visit split in two.

When should I book fall cleaning?

September or early October. October and November are the busiest gutter weeks of the year here and the calendar goes first-come, first-served. Booking early means you pick the week.

Do you haul the debris away?

Yes. Debris is bagged and leaves with us, and we clean up whatever landed on the ground below. No piles left at the curb or behind the garage.

What if I have gutter guards?

We can usually lift, clean under, and reseat most standard guard systems. Tell us what you have in the notes and we'll confirm before quoting — some proprietary systems we'll leave to the installer.
