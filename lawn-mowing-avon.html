We Know Avon Lawns

Avon has grown fast along the US 36 corridor, and most of its neighborhoods share the same lawn challenges. We deal with them every week.

Builder-Grade Turf on Clay

Newer Avon subdivisions sit on heavy central Indiana clay, and builder-installed lawns are often thin underneath. We mow high enough to protect the turf in summer heat instead of scalping it — the number one thing that keeps a young Avon lawn from burning out in July.

HOA-Ready Every Visit

From Prestwick to the newer developments off County Road 100, most Avon homes have HOA standards to meet. Trimmed edges, clean fence lines, and blown-off sidewalks are part of every cut, not an upsell.

Growth Spurts Handled

Avon lawns can jump several inches in a single spring week. Weekly and biweekly schedules are both available, and you can switch between them as the season changes — no contract to renegotiate.
