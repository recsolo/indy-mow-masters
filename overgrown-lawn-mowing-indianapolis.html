Why Overgrown Lawns Are Our Easiest Sell

Most mowing companies either turn tall grass away or quote scary "recovery" prices. We just charge a flat $20 more than a normal cut — once — because we'd rather earn a long-term customer than gouge a stressed-out one.

Transparent Price, Up Front

$40–$80 by yard size plus the one-time $20 overgrowth add-on. You know the number before we start the mower. After the first visit you pay the same standard rate as everyone else.

Cut Right, Not Just Cut

Dropping knee-high grass to two inches in one pass shocks the turf and leaves windrows of clippings everywhere. We take overgrowth down in stages within the visit so the lawn underneath survives the haircut.

Fast Turnaround

Quotes usually come back the same day, seven days a week. If you're working against a violation deadline or a home showing, say so — those cuts get priority.

Who Calls Us for Overgrown Lawns

Every summer we bring the same handful of situations back from the jungle:

Tall Grass Violation Notices

Indianapolis and the surrounding towns enforce grass-height rules, and HOAs are stricter still. One visit typically resolves the notice — and we can put you on a schedule so it never comes back.

Vacant & Rental Properties

Between tenants, mid-probate, or listed for sale — vacant homes announce themselves with tall grass. Landlords text us an address, we send back a quote and before/after confirmation.

Life Got in the Way

Injury, new baby, deployment, broken mower, or a lawn guy who ghosted mid-season. No lectures here — we cut it, clean up, and you decide if you want us back.

Overgrown Lawn FAQs

What people ask before booking a catch-up cut.

How much will my overgrown yard cost?

Standard rate for your yard size ($40/$60/$80) plus a one-time $20 add-on. Nothing else unless the lot has gone to heavy brush — and we'll tell you that before we come out, not after.

How fast can you get here?

Quotes usually same-day, seven days a week. Violation-notice and pre-showing cuts get priority scheduling.

Do you haul away the clippings?

We cut in stages and disperse clippings as evenly as the lawn allows, then blow all hard surfaces clean. Extremely heavy thatch situations are discussed in the quote.

Is this a one-time thing or do I have to sign up?

Entirely up to you. One-time cuts are welcome; if you'd like to stay caught up, weekly or biweekly service continues at standard rates with no contract.
