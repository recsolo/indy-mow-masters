Gutter Cleaning · Indianapolis

Clogged Gutters Are the Cheapest Problem You'll Ever Fix.

A packed gutter sends roof water down your siding and into the foundation. On Indianapolis clay, that is how wet basements and rotted fascia start — and one freeze turns the same debris into an ice dam.

We clear every run by hand, flush each downspout until it runs free, and take the mess with us. You get the exact price before we touch a ladder.

One-story homes only. We don't clean gutters on two-story or multi-level homes — that work needs fall-protection gear we don't carry. Not sure? Ask when you request your quote.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about pricing, scheduling, and what's included.

How much does lawn mowing cost in Indianapolis?

Simple flat rates by yard size: $40 small, $60 medium, $80 large. Every price includes mowing front and back, string trimming, and blower cleanup. Overgrown grass on the first visit adds a one-time $20.

How much does gutter cleaning cost in Indianapolis?

$99 to start for a small one-story home, $129 medium, $159 large. That covers clearing every gutter run, flushing the downspouts, and hauling the debris away. We clean one-story homes only and confirm your exact price before work begins. See gutter cleaning details.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. There are no contracts and nothing to cancel. Choose weekly or biweekly service, or book a one-time cut — your schedule, your call.

What's included in every mowing visit?

The full Mow & Go package every time: a complete mow of the front and back yard, trimming around edges, fences, trees, and obstacles, and a leaf blower cleanup of driveways, walkways, and patios.

Which areas do you serve?

Indianapolis, Avon, Plainfield, Brownsburg, Speedway, Whitestown, Zionsville, Fishers, Carmel, and Meridian Hills at standard rates. Outside that area, we just add $10 to your starting mowing price.

Do you have a cheaper mowing option?

Yes — our Economy Plan. We mow the front and back and blow off your property, but skip the trimming. Pricing is by request only: choose "Economy Plan" in the quote form or call and ask for a Money Saver quote.

How fast can I get a quote, and when can you start?

Quotes usually go out the same day, 7 days a week. Most new customers get their first visit within a few days — often sooner if you're on one of our existing routes. Send the form or call or text (317) 514-2861.

Can I get on the list for spring 2027?

Yes. The 2027 spring cleanup sign-up sheet is open now. Add your name and email and we'll contact you in late winter to confirm a date and a price. It's free and there's no obligation.
