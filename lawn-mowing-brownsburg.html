Two Kinds of Brownsburg Lawns — We Cut Both

Brownsburg splits into two very different mowing jobs: the older tree-lined streets near downtown and the wide-open newer subdivisions off Green Street and SR 267. Each needs a different approach.

Established Streets, Mature Trees

The neighborhoods around downtown and Arbuckle Acres Park have decades-old trees with surface roots and heavy shade. We trim around trunks by hand instead of scarring them with a mower deck, and cut shaded turf higher so it doesn't thin out.

Newer Subdivision Lots

The newer developments north and south of town are open, sunny, and fast-growing. These lawns need consistent weekly cuts through the spring flush — miss two weeks and you're in overgrown territory.

Leaf-Season Ready

All those mature Brownsburg trees mean serious leaf drop. When fall arrives we also offer leaf cleanup, so the same crew that mows your lawn can clear it.
